## Post Body

After 18 months of building, I'm proud to announce the launch of **LearningoPK** — a web-based learning platform purpose-built for 9th and 10th grade students across Pakistan's major examination boards.

Here's the reality that drove us to build this: Pakistan has over 60 million school-age children, yet fewer than 30% reach higher secondary education. By grade 10, the dropout rate exceeds 40%. The students who stay face a system where board-specific exam preparation is either inaccessible, unaffordable, or both.

A family in rural Sindh pays the same BISE examination fee as a family in Karachi, but the rural student has no access to a quality tuition center. That's not an education gap — it's an equity gap. And technology can close it.

**What LearningoPK delivers:**

1. **Board-aligned content** — Full chapter-wise solutions mapped to FBISE, Punjab BISE, and Sindh BISE syllabi. No more studying from the wrong board's material.

2. **AI Socratic Tutor** — A 24/7 AI teacher powered by Mistral AI that doesn't give answers. It asks questions. The Socratic method, at scale. Every student gets a personal tutor that guides them toward understanding, not memorization.

3. **Quizzes, Mock Exams & Flashcards with Spaced Repetition** — Designed by analyzing 5 years of board exam patterns. Our flashcards use a spaced repetition algorithm that schedules reviews at optimal intervals — the same technique used by medical students worldwide, now available to a 9th grader in Multan.

4. **Community & Gamification** — A student forum for peer discussion, study groups, XP points, leaderboards, and subject-wise progress tracking. Because motivation isn't just about content — it's about community.

5. **Past Papers & Revision Notes** — Organized by board, subject, and chapter. Searchable, always accessible.

**Freemium model:** Students can learn free with education-appropriate ads. Subscribe for PKR 200/month to remove ads and unlock premium features. Schools subscribe at PKR 500/student/year for ad-free institutional access with analytics and admin dashboards.

We built this on a modern stack — Next.js frontend, Express backend, PostgreSQL with Redis caching — because Pakistani students deserve infrastructure as good as any global EdTech platform.

LearningoPK is currently in MVP with comprehensive seed content across Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, Urdu, and Pakistan Studies.

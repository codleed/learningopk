# LinkedIn Post #15: Call to Action for School Partnerships

**Title:** We're Looking for 20 Schools to Pilot LearningoPK — Free, for One Full Term

**Target Audience:** Schools

**Best Posting Time:** Monday, 8:00 AM PKT

---

## Post Body

After 18 months of building, testing, and iterating, LearningoPK is ready for classrooms. But we're not looking to sell. We're looking to learn.

**We're offering a zero-cost pilot program to 20 schools and coaching centers across Pakistan.**

Here's what the pilot includes:

**Full platform access for one academic term (3–4 months):**
- Unlimited student accounts for your entire 9th and 10th grade cohort
- Teacher dashboard with student-level analytics, chapter-wise performance data, and engagement tracking
- Board-aligned content across all major subjects — FBISE, Punjab BISE, or Sindh BISE
- AI Socratic tutor, quizzes, mock exams, flashcards, and community forum
- School-wide leaderboard and gamification features
- Progress reports that you can share with parents

**What we're asking in return:**
Honest feedback. We want to know what works, what doesn't, and what would make LearningoPK genuinely indispensable for your teachers and students.

Specifically, we're looking for schools that:
- Have 9th and/or 10th grade sections
- Are affiliated with FBISE, any Punjab BISE, or any Sindh BISE
- Have internet access on campus (even if only in a computer lab)
- Have at least one teacher willing to integrate the platform into their teaching workflow and provide regular feedback
- Are interested in improving their exam results through technology — not just maintaining the status quo

**What the pilot commitment looks like for you:**
- **Minimal setup time.** We handle student account creation. You provide class lists.
- **Minimal teacher training.** A 45-minute onboarding session (virtual) for your teachers. The platform is designed to be intuitive.
- **Weekly check-ins (15 minutes).** A brief call or message exchange about what's working and what isn't.
- **End-of-term feedback session.** A 30-minute structured feedback conversation.

That's the entire commitment. No financial cost. No long-term obligation. No integration complexity.

**What your school gains:**
- A complete digital exam preparation platform — at zero cost during the pilot
- Data-driven visibility into student performance that no manual system can provide
- Early access to features and the ability to shape the product roadmap based on your actual needs
- Recognition as a pilot partner (with permission) — useful for your school's reputation as an early adopter of education technology
- If you continue after the pilot: PKR 500/student/year for ad-free institutional access with analytics and admin tools

**The type of schools we're prioritizing:**
We're not just looking for elite private schools in DHA and Clifton. We're looking for schools across the spectrum — government schools with motivated principals, mid-tier private schools, tuition academies in smaller cities, coaching centers in areas where board exam results need improvement. We want a pilot cohort that reflects the diversity of Pakistani education.

**How to apply:**
DM me here on LinkedIn with:
1. Your school's name, city, and board affiliation
2. Approximate number of 9th and 10th grade students
3. A sentence about why you're interested

I'll personally respond to every message within 48 hours. We're selecting 20 schools in total, and I want to make sure we get a diverse, committed cohort.

**If you're not a school but you know a principal, administrator, or academy owner who should see this — please tag them in the comments or share this post.**

Pakistan's education system doesn't change from the top down. It changes when individual schools make different decisions. This pilot is our attempt to start that process — one school at a time, with evidence, not promises.

**Let's build the future of exam preparation in Pakistan. Together.**

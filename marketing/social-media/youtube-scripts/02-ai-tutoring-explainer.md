## Production Notes

- **Tone:** This video should feel slightly more casual and engaging than the platform walkthrough. The topic is inherently interesting — sell the "aha!" moments. Let the demos breathe.
- **Pacing:** The demo sections (1:15–2:35) should feel unhurried. Don't speed through the AI-student dialogue. The viewer needs to follow the reasoning.
- **Captions:** Essential for this video. The chat messages are text, so burned-in captions for the voiceover prevent visual overload. Use a clean font that's distinct from the chat UI font.
- **Screen Recording:** Ensure the chat text is clearly readable at all device sizes. Zoom in on the chat during key exchanges. Keep the cursor visible to direct attention.
- **Music:** Slightly more engaging background track than the walkthrough video — something with a subtle "thinking" feel. Perhaps a soft piano or ambient electronic track with a steady rhythm.

That's the LearningoPK AI Tutor. Powered by Mistral AI, designed for Pakistani students.

**Here's how it's different from every other AI tool:**

Most AI chatbots give you answers. Ask ChatGPT "What is Newton's Third Law?" and you get a paragraph. You read it. Maybe you remember it. Maybe you don't. That's not teaching. That's an interactive encyclopedia.

Our AI tutor uses the **Socratic method** — the same approach Socrates used 2,500 years ago, and the same approach the world's best teachers use today.

You ask: "I don't understand Newton's Third Law."
It responds: "Let's think about this. When you push a wall, what do you feel?"
You: "I feel the wall pushing back?"
It: "Exactly. Now, if you push harder, what changes?"
You: "The wall pushes back harder?"
It: "You're describing Newton's Third Law right now. Can you state it in your own words?"

**See what happened there?** The AI didn't tell you the law. It guided you to discover it yourself. That's learning that sticks — because you arrived at the answer through your own reasoning, not through memorization.

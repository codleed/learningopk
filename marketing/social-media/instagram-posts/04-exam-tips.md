## Caption

After analyzing 5 years of board exam papers and talking to dozens of teachers, we've identified the same mistakes appearing again and again in student answer sheets. These aren't knowledge gaps — they're strategy gaps. And they're costing students marks they could easily keep.

Here are the top 5 — swipe to see the fixes. 👉

**1. Writing everything you know instead of what's asked**
The question asks for three differences between X and Y. You write seven — hoping the examiner finds three correct ones among them. Strategy: Examiners mark your first three points. If the first three are wrong, you lose marks even if point #4 is right. Read the question. Answer exactly what's asked. No more, no less.

**2. Ignoring the marks-to-time ratio**
A 2-mark short question should take 3–4 minutes. A 10-mark long question should take 12–15 minutes. Students often spend 20 minutes perfecting a 3-mark question and then rush through the 10-mark one. Use the reading time (first 15 minutes) to plan your time allocation per section.

**3. Skipping the diagram/practical component**
In Physics, Chemistry, and Biology, labeled diagrams are worth 20–30% of long question marks. A correct diagram with labels can save a partially correct written answer. Practice drawing and labeling key diagrams — they're free marks if you prepare them.

**4. Not practicing WITH a timer**
You can solve every question in the past paper when you're relaxed at home. But can you solve them in 2 hours and 45 minutes with exam pressure? The only way to know is to practice under timed conditions. LearningoPK's mock exams include configurable timers — use them.

**5. Studying the wrong board's material**
Sounds obvious, but it happens constantly. A student under BISE Bahawalpur studies from notes prepared for BISE Lahore. Chapters are ordered differently. Some topics are missing. Some are extra. Always verify your study material matches YOUR board's specific syllabus. LearningoPK handles this automatically — select your board once, and all content is aligned.

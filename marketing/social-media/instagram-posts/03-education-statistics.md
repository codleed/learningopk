## Caption

The numbers behind Pakistan's education system are staggering. But numbers without action are just trivia. Numbers with a solution become a mission. 🎯

Swipe through to see what we're up against — and what we're building to change it. 👉

**60.2 million** school-age children in Pakistan. That's larger than the population of Italy, Spain, or South Korea.

**22.8 million** are out of school entirely. The second-highest number in the world.

**41%** of students who start 9th grade don't make it to their 10th-grade board exams. They drop out before the finish line.

**$3.2 billion** is spent annually on private tuition by Pakistani families. Education is theoretically free. In practice, it's one of the largest household expenses.

**Only 29%** of students reach higher secondary education (grades 11–12). 71% of young Pakistanis never get past matric.

These numbers don't exist because Pakistani students aren't capable. They exist because the system doesn't give them what they need — board-specific content, personalized guidance, and tools that work with their reality, not against it.

LearningoPK is our answer. Free. Board-specific. AI-powered. Built for Pakistani students from day one.

**We can't change 60 million outcomes overnight. But we can start with one student, one chapter, one quiz at a time.**

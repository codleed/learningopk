## Caption

Not all quizzes are created equal.

A generic "Class 10 Physics Quiz" tests random facts from random syllabi. It doesn't know your board. It doesn't match your exam pattern. It doesn't tell you what to do with your results.

LearningoPK quizzes are different — because we built them from actual board exam data. 📊

**Here's what makes them powerful:**

🎯 **Board-Specific Question Banks**
Every question is tagged to your specific board — FBISE, Punjab BISE, or Sindh BISE. No more "Wait, was this even in our syllabus?" moments.

📐 **Real Exam Pattern Distribution**
We analyzed 5 years of past papers. Our quizzes mirror the exact question type distribution of your board's exams — same MCQ/short/long question ratio, same chapter weightage, same difficulty distribution.

📊 **Instant, Actionable Feedback**
You don't just get a score. You get a breakdown: which chapters are strong, which subtopics need work, what types of questions you consistently get wrong. The quiz isn't the end — it's the beginning of targeted improvement.

🔄 **Adaptive Retesting**
Score 70% on a chapter quiz? The platform generates a follow-up quiz focusing on just the topics you missed. Retake it. Score 90%. Now you've actually learned — not just passed a test.

⏱️ **Timed Mock Exams**
Full-length mock exams with configurable timers that simulate actual board exam conditions. Practice under pressure so the real thing feels familiar.

📈 **Progress Tracking Over Time**
Your quiz scores are plotted over weeks and months. See your improvement curve. Know that you're getting better — with data, not gut feeling.

**Ready to test yourself?** Pick your board, choose a chapter, and take a quiz — all in under 5 minutes. Link in bio. 🇵🇰

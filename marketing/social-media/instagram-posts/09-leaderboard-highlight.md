## Caption

What if studying for board exams felt less like a chore and more like a challenge you actually want to win? 🎮

That's the idea behind the LearningoPK leaderboard. Not empty gamification. Smart motivation design.

**Here's how it works:**

🏆 **XP for Effort, Not Just Results**
You earn XP for the actions that lead to learning — completing quizzes, maintaining streaks, asking thoughtful questions to the AI tutor, helping classmates in the forum. The leaderboard rewards consistency, not just natural ability.

📊 **Segmented by Subject**
Global leaderboards demotivate 90% of students. Ours are segmented by subject (Chemistry, Math, Physics...) and by chapter. You might not be #1 overall — but you could be #1 in "Chemistry — Chapter 5." That micro-achievement matters.

🔥 **Streak Tracking**
A visible streak counter tracks your consecutive days of platform activity. Day 7. Day 14. Day 30. Each milestone is celebrated. Breaking a long streak is painful — and that's exactly the point. Daily habits are built on not wanting to break the chain.

👥 **Study Group Leaderboards**
Form a group with your classmates. Set a group goal. Compete together. Peer accountability, supercharged. When your friend sees you haven't logged in today, they'll message you. That's motivation no notification can match.

**The psychology behind it:**
Gamification works when it reinforces intrinsic motivation, not replaces it. No student uses LearningoPK *because* of XP. They use it because the content is good and the AI tutor actually helps. The gamification is the extra push — the reason they do one more quiz instead of closing the app.

**Check your rank.** Link in bio. 🇵🇰

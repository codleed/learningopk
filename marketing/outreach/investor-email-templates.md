## Usage Guidelines

| Element | Guideline |
|---|---|
| **Cold outreach timing** | Send Tuesday through Thursday, 9:00 AM–11:00 AM PKT for best open rates |
| **Follow-up cadence** | Templates 1 and 2: follow up after 5 business days if no response. Template 3: follow up within 24-48 hours after the pitch meeting. Template 4: send on the 1st-3rd of each month consistently |
| **Investor deck** | Always attach or link the current investor deck when following up after a pitch meeting (Template 3). For cold outreach (Template 1), mention the deck but do not attach it — offer to send it upon interest to avoid spam filters |
| **Monthly updates** | Even if an investor has not committed, keep sending updates for 6 months. Consistent, substantive updates build credibility and keep you top-of-mind. If they ask to stop, remove them immediately |
| **Tone** | Templates 1 and 2: confident and data-forward. Template 3: grateful and substantive. Template 4: transparent and momentum-driven |
| **Metrics hygiene** | Never fabricate or inflate metrics. If a metric declined, acknowledge it honestly with a brief explanation. Investors value transparency far more than perfection |

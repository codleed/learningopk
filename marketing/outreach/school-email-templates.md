## Usage Guidelines

| Element | Guideline |
|---|---|
| **[Bracketed Fields]** | Replace with recipient-specific information before sending |
| **Subject Line Choice** | Use Primary by default; A/B test across batches of 50+ recipients to measure open rates |
| **Follow-Up Timing** | Send exactly 5 business days after the initial email. If no response after follow-up, wait 30 days before re-engaging |
| **Tone Calibration** | Template 1 & 5: deferential and respectful (school principals/network heads). Template 2: collegial and direct (academy owners). Template 3: warm and personal (referrals). Template 4: energetic and specific (event connections) |
| **Attachment Strategy** | Always attach the 1-page platform overview PDF referenced in templates. Do not attach large files in the first email — offer to share the investor deck or detailed proposal only after a positive response |
| **Tracking** | Use email tracking (opens, clicks) to prioritize follow-ups. A recipient who opened 3+ times but did not reply is a high-priority follow-up candidate |

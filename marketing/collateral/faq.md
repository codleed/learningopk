# LearningoPK — Frequently Asked Questions

---

## General Questions

### 1. What is LearningoPK?

LearningoPK is a free, web-based learning platform built specifically for 9th and 10th grade Pakistani students. It covers Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Mathematics aligned to the Federal Board (FBISE), Punjab BISE, and Sindh BISE syllabi. The platform combines chapter-wise solutions, a 24/7 AI-powered Socratic tutor, quizzes, mock exams, flashcards, progress tracking, and a community forum — all in one place, accessible from any device.

### 2. Is LearningoPK really free? How do you make money?

LearningoPK uses a sustainable freemium model. Students can learn free with education-appropriate display ads. Students who prefer an ad-free experience with premium features (unlimited AI tutoring, advanced analytics, unlimited mock exams) can subscribe for PKR 200/month. Schools and institutions purchase annual subscriptions at PKR 500/student/year for ad-free institutional access with admin dashboards, analytics, and management tools. We never sell user data. Our mission is to make quality exam preparation accessible to every Pakistani student, regardless of their financial situation.

### 3. Who created LearningoPK?

LearningoPK was built by a team of Pakistani engineers, educators, and designers who experienced firsthand the stress and financial burden of the Pakistani exam preparation system. Our team combines expertise in software development (Next.js, Express, PostgreSQL, Redis, MinIO), AI/ML, and pedagogy. We're headquartered in Pakistan and deeply committed to improving educational outcomes across the country.

### 4. Which boards and grades do you cover?

We currently cover grades 9 and 10 (Matriculation) across three major Pakistani examination boards: Federal Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (FBISE), Punjab Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (BISE), and Sindh Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (BISE). The platform is designed so that students select their board and grade upon signup, and all content — chapter solutions, quizzes, and past papers — is automatically aligned to their specific curriculum.

### 5. How is LearningoPK different from Khan Academy or Sabaq.pk?

LearningoPK uniquely combines what each of these platforms offers individually. Khan Academy has excellent AI tutoring (Khanmigo) but charges for it and doesn't align to Pakistani curricula. Sabaq.pk has free, board-aligned video content but no AI tutor, no quizzes, and no spaced repetition system. We deliver board-specific content, an AI tutor, adaptive quizzes, spaced repetition flashcards, gamification, and community features — all on one platform with an ad-supported free tier. No competitor currently offers this combination.

## Questions for Students

### 6. How do I sign up?

Signing up takes less than 60 seconds. Visit www.learningopk.com, click "Sign Up," and enter your email address or phone number. You'll be asked to select your board (FBISE, Punjab BISE, or Sindh BISE), grade (9th or 10th), and the subjects you're studying. After that, you're ready to start. No credit card, no payment information — just a commitment to learning.

### 7. Do I need a fast internet connection?

LearningoPK is designed to work on moderate connections, but an internet connection is required since it's a web-based platform. The Progressive Web App (PWA) architecture caches recently accessed content so that pages load faster on repeat visits, and our AI tutor is optimized for responsiveness even on slower networks. That said, a stable connection of at least 2 Mbps is recommended for the best experience, particularly for AI tutor interactions. Full offline support is on our product roadmap.

### 8. Can I use LearningoPK on my phone?

Absolutely. LearningoPK is a Progressive Web App (PWA), which means it works on any device with a web browser — smartphone, tablet, laptop, or desktop. On mobile, you can even install it to your home screen for a native app-like experience. We've designed every feature, from the AI tutor to the community forum, to be fully responsive and touch-friendly on smaller screens.

### 9. How does the AI tutor work?

Our AI tutor is powered by Mistral AI and uses the Socratic method — it doesn't just give you answers. When you ask a question, the tutor guides you toward the solution by asking clarifying questions, breaking down problems into smaller steps, and prompting you to think critically. You can ask it anything related to your 9th or 10th grade subjects, including conceptual questions, problem-solving help, and exam strategy. It's available 24/7, so you're never stuck — even at 11 PM the night before an exam.

### 10. How do I track my progress?

Your personal dashboard shows everything at a glance: which chapters you've completed, your quiz scores, your current XP and leaderboard rank, your daily streak, and a visual breakdown of your strengths and weaknesses per subject. The platform also provides a "Predicted Exam Readiness" score that uses your performance data to estimate how well-prepared you are for your board exams. This helps you focus your study time on the topics that need the most attention.

## Questions for Parents

### 11. Is the content aligned with my child's board syllabus?

Yes — content alignment is the foundation of LearningoPK. Every chapter solution, quiz question, flashcard, and past paper is mapped to the official textbooks and syllabi of FBISE, Punjab BISE, and Sindh BISE. When your child selects their board during signup, the platform automatically serves content aligned to their specific curriculum. We regularly update our content to reflect syllabus changes announced by any of the covered boards.

### 12. Is the AI tutor safe for my child?

Yes, we take safety seriously. The AI tutor is designed to stay strictly within academic boundaries — it will only discuss topics related to your child's subjects and will not engage with inappropriate, personal, or off-topic conversations. All tutor interactions are logged for quality assurance, and the system is tested against safety benchmarks. Additionally, the Socratic teaching approach encourages critical thinking and understanding rather than passive answer-copying, which supports genuine learning.

### 13. Will LearningoPK replace my child's tuition teacher?

LearningoPK is designed to complement, not necessarily replace, a child's learning ecosystem. For many students, especially those who cannot afford tuition or need help outside tuition hours, it can serve as a complete study companion. For others, it works alongside tuition — reinforcing what's taught in class, providing extra practice through quizzes and flashcards, and offering instant help when a teacher isn't available. We encourage parents to view it as a powerful supplement that ensures no child is ever stuck without academic support.

### 14. How do I know my child is actually learning?

The progress dashboard provides transparent, data-driven insights into your child's learning. You can see time spent studying, chapter completion rates, quiz scores, and topic-level strengths and weaknesses. The "Predicted Exam Readiness" score gives a clear, quantitative signal of preparation level. We're also building parent-specific dashboards so that parents can monitor progress without needing to log into their child's account directly. In the meantime, we encourage parents to review progress alongside their child.

### 15. Is my child's data private?

Yes. We take data privacy seriously and comply with applicable Pakistani data protection standards. We never sell or share student data with third parties. Account information and learning data are stored securely using industry-standard encryption. The platform does not display personally identifiable information publicly — leaderboards and forum posts use usernames, not real names. For a detailed breakdown, please see our Privacy Policy at www.learningopk.com/privacy (coming soon).

## Questions for Schools & Institutions

### 16. Can our entire school use LearningoPK?

Yes — our school subscription program is designed for exactly this. Schools can onboard their entire student body (grades 9 and 10) onto the platform with managed accounts, custom content integration, and a dedicated institutional admin dashboard for school leadership. The platform is student-first — there are no individual teacher accounts. Instead, the institutional dashboard provides school administrators with cohort-wide analytics and management tools. We offer flexible pricing at PKR 500/student/year with bulk discounts for larger networks. Contact schools@learningopk.com to schedule a demo.

### 17. Do you offer training for school staff?

Yes. As part of our school subscription program, we provide onboarding workshops and orientation sessions for school leadership and staff to help them integrate LearningoPK into their school's study culture. This includes training on using the institutional analytics dashboard to identify struggling students, understanding student progress reports, and leveraging the platform to support both in-class and at-home learning. Training can be conducted in-person or virtually depending on location. Note: the platform is student-first — there are no individual teacher accounts. School administrators access the institutional dashboard to monitor student progress.

### 18. How does LearningoPK integrate with our existing curriculum?

If your school follows the FBISE, Punjab BISE, or Sindh BISE textbooks, LearningoPK maps directly to your existing curriculum with zero integration effort — students simply access the relevant chapters on the platform. For schools using supplementary materials or alternative textbooks, we can work with your academic team to map supplementary content onto our platform. Custom content hosting and private school-specific question banks are available under our enterprise subscription tier.

### 19. Is there an admin dashboard for school management?

Yes. Our institutional admin dashboard provides school leadership with class-level analytics: average quiz scores, chapter completion rates across the cohort, identification of students who are falling behind, time-on-platform metrics, and subject-level performance breakdowns. School administrators can manage student accounts (bulk creation, password resets, grade/board assignment), view school-wide usage reports, and export data for internal reporting or parent-teacher meetings.

### 20. What does the school pilot program involve?

Our school pilot program is a low-commitment, low-risk way for schools to evaluate LearningoPK. We onboard one or two classrooms (typically 30–60 students) for a 4- to 8-week trial period at no cost. During the pilot, we provide orientation for school leadership, student onboarding support, and weekly check-ins to gather feedback. At the end of the pilot, we present a summary report showing student engagement metrics, performance improvement data, and administrator feedback to help the school decide whether to subscribe for ongoing access.

## Questions for Investors

### 21. What stage is LearningoPK at?

LearningoPK has a fully built and deployed Minimum Viable Product (MVP) with seed content covering Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Mathematics for grades 9–10 across FBISE, Punjab BISE, and Sindh BISE. The platform is functional end-to-end — students can sign up, access chapter content, interact with the AI tutor, take quizzes, use flashcards, and participate in the forum. We are currently in the pre-revenue stage, focused on user acquisition, product refinement based on early feedback, and establishing initial school partnerships.

### 22. What's your business model?

Our business model has three revenue streams. **Primary: Ad Revenue.** The free tier is supported by education-appropriate display advertising. **Second: Individual Subscriptions.** Students can subscribe for PKR 200/month for an ad-free experience with premium features (unlimited AI tutoring, advanced analytics, unlimited mock exams). **Third: School Subscriptions.** Schools and educational institutions pay PKR 500/student/year for ad-free institutional access, admin dashboards, analytics, and management tools. This freemium model ensures every student can access the platform while generating sustainable revenue from those who can pay.

### 23. What's your user acquisition strategy?

Our acquisition strategy operates on three tracks. **Track 1 — Organic/Community:** Social media content targeting students (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts), exam-season campaigns (e.g., "30 Days to Top Marks"), and word-of-mouth through the community forum and study groups. **Track 2 — School Partnerships:** Direct outreach to schools in Islamabad, Lahore, and Karachi for pilot programs, with the goal of converting pilots into paid B2B contracts and gaining built-in student user bases. **Track 3 — Strategic Partnerships:** Collaborations with educational NGOs, tuition center networks, and board-affiliated organizations to reach students through trusted intermediaries.

### 24. What's your competitive moat?

Our competitive moat has four layers. **First: Content specificity.** Board-aligned content for three Pakistani examination boards is time-intensive to build and maintain — it requires deep curriculum expertise that serves as a meaningful barrier to entry for international platforms. **Second: Integrated AI + pedagogy.** The Socratic AI tutor, SM-2 spaced repetition, and exam pattern analysis together create a personalized learning loop that improves with usage data — becoming harder to replicate over time. **Third: Network effects.** The community forum, study groups, and gamified leaderboards become more valuable as the user base grows, creating switching costs for engaged students. **Fourth: Mission alignment.** Our "free forever for students" commitment, combined with being a Pakistan-founded company, builds trust and loyalty that purely commercial competitors cannot easily match.

### 25. How do you plan to scale?

Our scaling roadmap has three phases. **Phase 1 (2026):** Consolidate the existing platform by expanding user base in our current target audience (grades 9–10, three boards), refine features based on data and feedback, and secure initial B2B school contracts to validate the revenue model. **Phase 2 (2027):** Expand content coverage to grades 8 and 11 across all three boards, add support for Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP) and Balochistan boards, and introduce offline mode via enhanced PWA capabilities. **Phase 3 (2028+):** Complete K-12 coverage across all Pakistani boards, launch native mobile apps, explore expansion into O-Level and A-Level curricula, and evaluate international expansion into structurally similar South Asian markets (Bangladesh, Sri Lanka).

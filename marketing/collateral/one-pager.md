Start free with ads, or subscribe for an ad-free premium experience.

---

## The Problem

- **78% of Pakistani secondary students rely on private tuition**, costing families PKR 3,000–15,000 per month — an enormous burden for low- and middle-income households. *(Source: ASER Pakistan)*
- **Existing online platforms are either too generic** (not aligned to FBISE, Punjab, or Sindh board syllabi), too expensive, or built for international audiences — leaving Pakistani students underserved.
- **Students lack personalized, instant academic help.** A student stuck on a physics problem at 11 PM has nowhere to turn — tuition centers are closed, and generic YouTube videos don't address their specific board's exam patterns.

---

## Our Solution

- **Board-specific, curriculum-aligned content** — Every chapter solution, quiz, and past paper is mapped to FBISE, Punjab BISE, and Sindh BISE syllabi for 9th and 10th grade.
- **24/7 AI Socratic Tutor** — Powered by Mistral AI, our tutor doesn't just give answers; it guides students through problems step-by-step, building conceptual understanding anytime, anywhere.
- **Complete learning ecosystem** — Quizzes, mock exams, spaced-repetition flashcards (SM-2 algorithm), formula library (LaTeX), community forum, study groups, XP leaderboards, and progress analytics — all under one roof, accessible with an ad-supported free tier.

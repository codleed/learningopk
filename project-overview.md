# 1. Project Overview & Goals

LearningoPK is a web-based learning platform targeting 9th and 10th
grade students in Pakistan (Federal, Punjab, and Sindh boards). The
platform provides chapter-wise solutions, AI-powered Socratic teaching,
quizzes, mock exams, flashcards, a student community forum, and
subject-wise progress tracking --- all runnable locally at zero cost for
investor demonstration purposes.

## Core Objectives

- Provide free, board-specific chapter solutions for all major
  subjects

- Offer an AI teacher that guides students through problems using the
  Socratic method (asking questions rather than giving direct answers)

- Enable self-assessment through quizzes, mock exams, and flashcards
  per chapter

- Build a community forum where students discuss problems and share
  tips

- Track each student\'s progress across subjects, chapters, and quiz
  scores

- Demonstrate to investors a fully working MVP with real content and
  real AI interaction
